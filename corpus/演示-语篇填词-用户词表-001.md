# 语篇填词实测：词表 → 六级风格原创语篇

## 实测目标

验证 `modules/cloze.md` 的核心闭环：词表建模 → 主题先行 → 空位与干扰项设计 → 独立求解 → 唯一性审计 → 中文式复盘。

这篇材料为原创训练文本，不仿写任何一套真题，不使用外刊或真题连续句子。

## 用户作答版（答案先隔离）

Read the passage first. Choose one word for each blank; use each word only once. Change the form only when grammar requires it.

Universities often launch ambitious digital-learning projects, but the success of a platform depends less on its appearance than on the evidence behind it. Before expanding a pilot, administrators should first ______ (1) funds to questions that can be tested rather than to features that merely look impressive. They should also collect ______ (2) measurements from controlled observations instead of relying on enthusiastic testimonials. Otherwise, a hidden ______ (3) in who responds to a survey may make a weak program appear effective.

Even good data do not speak for themselves. A research team must ______ (4) a clear explanation from several imperfect observations and make sure that its account is ______ (5). If the platform is introduced under a severe time ______ (6), the team should say so openly. Student participation may ______ (7) from week to week, and a reminder message can sometimes ______ (8) the very habit it is intended to measure. Such effects do not make the project useless; they show why a single impressive result is not enough.

The long-term test is whether the design is ______ (9). A single semester can produce promising signals, but it is ______ (10) impossible to judge the platform's value from that evidence alone.

### 选项

A. allocate  B. bias  C. coherent  D. constraint  E. derive  F. empirical  G. fluctuate  H. incentive  I. reinforce  J. shift  K. substantial  L. sustainable  M. undergo  N. virtually  O. obscure

## 独立求解记录

在答案键隔离的第二个回合中，按“词性 → 局部搭配 → 篇章逻辑”求解：

`1-A, 2-F, 3-B, 4-E, 5-C, 6-D, 7-G, 8-I, 9-L, 10-N`

所有空均获得单一最佳答案；未使用选项字母分布、词长或首字母捷径。

## 唯一性审计

- (1) `allocate funds to`：动词及物结构和资源语义共同约束；`derive` 不能接 funds。
- (2) `empirical measurements`：形容词修饰 measurements，且 `controlled observations` 提供“来自观察/实验的实证”约束；`coherent` 不能表达这个来源属性。
- (3) `hidden bias`：调查回应者构成造成系统性偏差；其他名词不能自然解释 survey distortion。
- (4) `derive ... from observations`：固定论证搭配；`undergo` 无法带 explanation 作宾语。
- (5) `coherent account`：篇章要求解释连贯；`sustainable` 与 account 的语义不配。
- (6) `under a severe time constraint`：介词和名词搭配直接锁定。
- (7) `participation may fluctuate`：情态动词后用原形，且语义是周间波动。
- (8) `reinforce the habit`：提醒可能强化被测行为；`fluctuate` 不及物，不能接 habit。
- (9) `sustainable` 修饰 `design`，回应“长期测试”；`substantial` 只说明数量/程度，不能表达可持续性。
- (10) `virtually impossible`：副词修饰 `impossible`，表达“几乎不可能仅凭一学期判断”；`substantial` 是形容词，不能占据该副词槽位。

## 难度与出题质量记录

- 文章长度：约 240 词；10 空、15 选项，模拟六级常见的 5 个未使用干扰项。
- 约束类型：局部搭配 5 个、词性/句法 3 个、跨句逻辑 2 个。
- 干扰项：没有用生僻词制造难度；近义竞争主要通过搭配、语域和论证方向消解。
- 证据距离：第 3、8、10 空需要回看前后句；其余多为句内或邻句证据。
- 风险标记：第 2 空改为 `empirical measurements from controlled observations` 后，搭配和来源约束更强；正式发布前仍应进行人工二审。

## 中文式复盘输出模板

用户提交答案后，每空按以下顺序反馈：

1. 先说词性和句法槽位；
2. 再说最小搭配证据；
3. 再说前后句的逻辑作用；
4. 最后解释最强干扰项为何被排除；
5. 对错题生成一个不复用原句的最小变式，并安排间隔复现。
