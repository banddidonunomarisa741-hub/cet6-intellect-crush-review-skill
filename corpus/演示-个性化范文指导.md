# 个性化范文指导演示（原创练习材料）

本演示只说明输出形态，不是官方真题或官方范文。假设用户语言库中有：

```text
be conducive to | 有助于；促进 | active
strike a balance between A and B | 在 A 与 B 之间取得平衡 | recycle
What matters is not A but B | 重要的不是 A 而是 B | recycle
```

## 题目（原创）

Some people believe that university students should spend more time developing digital skills. Write an essay expressing your view.

## 个性化选择

- `be conducive to`：可自然连接“数字技能对学习/就业的作用”；
- `strike a balance between A and B`：适合第二段处理技能训练与基础学习的权衡；
- `What matters is not A but B`：适合结尾强调目标不是追逐工具，而是有效解决问题。

没有把三个表达机械重复；每个表达只承担一次明确的论证功能。

## 自然英文范文

Digital skills are increasingly important for university students, but they should be developed for a clear purpose. Used wisely, these skills can be **conducive to** more efficient learning and can help students evaluate information in a critical way.

Universities should therefore **strike a balance between** technical training and the cultivation of fundamental abilities. Students still need to read carefully, communicate clearly and solve unfamiliar problems. **What matters is not** how many tools a student can operate **but** whether those tools lead to better thinking and responsible action.

In my view, digital literacy should be treated as a means rather than an end. With suitable guidance and regular practice, students can turn technology into a practical resource without allowing it to replace independent judgment.

## 亲民词句注释

| 表达 | 此处中文义 | 词性/句型槽位 | 搭配与限制 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| be conducive to | 有助于；促进 | 形容词短语；`be conducive to + noun/gerund` | `to` 是介词，后接名词或动名词，不接动词原形 | contribute to; facilitate |
| strike a balance between A and B | 在 A 与 B 之间取得平衡 | 动词短语；A/B 尽量保持平行 | 不要写成 `balance A to B` | balance A with B |
| What matters is not A but B | 重要的不是 A，而是 B | 强调结构；A/B 需语法和语义平行 | `not ... but ...` 两侧不能一个是名词、另一个是完整从句而无意图 | The key point is that ... |
| evaluate information | 评估信息、判断信息是否可靠 | `evaluate + noun` | 比 `know information` 更正式；不等于“接受信息” | assess information |
| a means rather than an end | 手段而非目的 | 名词短语 | 常用于抽象论证；`means` 此处是“手段” | a tool, not the goal |

## 用户复用任务

1. 用 `be conducive to` 改写一句关于阅读训练的话。
2. 把 `strike a balance between` 的 A/B 换成“效率”和“准确性”，保持平行结构。
3. 不看范文，用 `What matters is not A but B` 写一个与你专业相关的句子。
